**Artifact of Distribution** solves a specific problem: item hoarding.

I'm a terrible loot goblin. I ninja loot at pretty much every opportunity presented to me.

This mod solves the problem that I created. Everyone gets their own copy of dropped items now.

## How It Works

![Toggling the mod's function from the character screen.](/upload/artifact_of_distribution.mp4)
 
The mod doesn't immediately engage with the game. It's an artifact that you either turn on, or turn off before the run starts.

![Everyone gets their own item. Nobody can steal your share.](/upload/instanced_items.mp4)

The mod is designed with <a href="https://riskofrain2.fandom.com/wiki/Artifact_of_Command">Artifact of Command</a> in mind. Command Essences provide you with 3 options instead of the entire tier pool.

You're offered the chance to reroll your choices at the cost of gold.

## Balancing

I've done a decent job at balancing this out.

1. Dropped items from the Teleporter are distributed evenly. This applies to Shrine of the Mountain as well.
2. Lunar Coins are not instanced. This mod is trying to solve teammates out-scaling you, not make everyone filthy rich.
3. Purchased items are instanced, but every player must pay to unlock their instance of the item. (i.e: <a href="https://riskofrain2.fandom.com/wiki/Void_Cradle">Void Cradles</a>)
